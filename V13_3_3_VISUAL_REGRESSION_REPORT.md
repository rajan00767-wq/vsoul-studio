## 1. Context & Motivation

In V13.3.2, automated test harnesses verified 22/22 quality gates and delivered 7/7 test cases with zero automated defects. However, human visual inspection rejected all seven photographs:
- **PA_01**: Red collar mostly erased, unnatural neckline, dark shoulder outline.
- **PA_02**: Rectangular neck patch visible, navy collar partially erased.
- **PA_03**: Left maroon bow missing, rectangular chest seam, missing tie knot, black shoulder outlines.
- **PA_04**: Neckline unnatural, shoulder boundary unnatural, floral hair bows missed.
- **PA_05**: Red collar partially erased, unnatural neck integration, shoulder artifacts.
- **PA_11**: Black shoulder outlines and chin seam remained.
- **PA_12**: Red collar incomplete, unnatural neckline, lower sleeve/background artifacts.

The failure was structural: the legacy compositing pipeline destroyed the realistic garment geometry generated by Qwen by flat-pasting an unaligned, unscaled source face/neck directly over the collar, and Lanczos resampling against transparent black introduced stark dark outlines along the shoulders.

---

## 2. Root Cause Investigation & Solutions

### A. Face Scale & Position Divergence
- **Cause**: In raw Qwen candidates, the face height is ~365 px ($chin \approx 520$), while the normalized source student face height is ~490 px ($chin \approx 712$). Direct flat-pasting positioned the source neck 180 px lower into the canvas, directly obliterating the collar opening and tie knot.
- **Solution**: Developed `pipelines/uniform_swap/garment_registrar.py`. Computes 2D similarity transform matrix $M$ from candidate landmarks to source student anatomy, decomposes the garment into collar lapels, tie knot, opening, and body, warps the garment onto the student's chest, and confines throat skin strictly within the collar opening.

### B. Black Shoulder Outlines
- **Cause**: Resampling with transparent black padding `(0, 0, 0, 0)` caused Lanczos downsampling to average edge pixels with `(0, 0, 0)`, creating high-contrast black contours around the shoulders. Telea inpainting sampled from these black boundary pixels.
- **Solution**: Updated `produce_passport_crop` in `pipelines/uniform_swap/image_normalizer.py` to pad canvases with `(*background_rgb, 0)`. Added color-bleeding pre-conditioning before downsampling.

### C. Layer Occlusion Priority
- **Cause**: Neck skin and chest regions were layered over garment components.
- **Solution**: Implemented depth-correct 7-layer compositing in `_composite_v13_3_3`:
  1. Layer 1: Garment Body
  2. Layer 2: Dynamic Throat Skin
  3. Layer 3: Registered Collar Lapels & Tie Knot
  4. Layer 4: Face Core & Ears
  5. Layer 5: Scalp Hair
  6. Layer 6: Long Hair & Braids
  7. Layer 7: Tracked Individual Accessories
